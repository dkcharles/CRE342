Week 3

Rewards, feedback & playing fair

Designing reward systems: reward types, short- and long-term, tiered and milestone rewards, fast and slow loops and reinforcement schedules; feedback that is immediate, layered and accessible; gamification beyond points, badges and leaderboards; and the ethics of loot boxes and other reward dark patterns. In the practical you play Roll for Reward, then design your own reward system.

Open the slides Take the quiz

Slides: use the arrow keys or swipe; press F for full screen and Esc for an overview.

By the end of this week you can
  • Design a reward system using short- and long-term, tiered, milestone, fixed and random rewards in fast and slow loops.
  • Design feedback that is immediate, clear, layered across the senses and accessible.
  • Separate game design from gamification, and explain why points, badges and leaderboards alone aren't enough.
  • Judge a reward system's ethics and redesign a dark pattern so it respects the player.
