Week 2

Flow, immersion & what breaks them

From mechanics to experience (MDA and Schell's tetrad), the flow channel, three sources of immersion, agency and presence, and the distractors that break them. In the practical you play Juggle Circle and measure your own flow.

Open the slides Take the quiz

Slides: use the arrow keys or swipe; press F for full screen and Esc for an overview.

By the end of this week you can
  • Explain flow using the challenge–skill channel, and name at least four of its conditions.
  • Distinguish sensory, challenge-based and imaginative immersion, and the barriers that block each.
  • Classify distractors and predict which ones break flow for which player type and genre.
  • Design a simple test with a baseline, a manipulation and a self-report measure.
Before next week

Before the practical, know your top Hexad type (12-question test). Afterwards, type your three rows into the class Google Sheet (Players tab), using your player ID or initials, not your full name.
