Week 1

Player psychology, motivation & behaviour

How players perceive, think and remember; who plays (Bartle, Yee, Quantic Foundry); why they play (Maslow, self-determination theory, intrinsic and extrinsic motivation); and how design shapes behaviour, including where it turns into dark patterns.

Open the slides Take the quiz

Slides: use the arrow keys or swipe; press F for full screen and Esc for an overview.

By the end of this week you can
  • Explain how perception, decision-making, problem-solving and memory shape play.
  • Compare Bartle's player types with Yee's components and the Quantic Foundry profile.
  • Apply self-determination theory and intrinsic vs extrinsic motivation to real mechanics.
  • Judge how games predict, influence and encourage behaviour, and when that becomes a dark pattern.
